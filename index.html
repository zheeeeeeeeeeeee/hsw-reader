HSW reader

A small private tool for one YouTube channel, run by the channel's owner.

What it is

HSW reader is a set of scripts the channel's owner runs on their own computer. It is not a public service and has no accounts, sign-ups or other users. It is used with one YouTube channel, the owner's own.

What it does

  • Reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, traffic sources, audience retention) so the owner can review how each video did.
  • Lists public comments on the channel's videos.
  • Posts replies to those comments, only replies the channel's owner has written or approved, one at a time.

What it does not do

It does not upload, edit or delete videos, and it does not collect data about viewers beyond what YouTube already shows the channel's owner.
