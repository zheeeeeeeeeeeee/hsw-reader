Privacy policy

HSW reader. Last updated 10 October 2026.

HSW reader is a private tool used only by the owner of one YouTube channel, on the owner's own computer. Nobody else can sign in to it.

Data it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, through Google's YouTube APIs, it accesses:

  • the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports and video details;
  • public comments on the channel's videos (the comment text, the commenter's public channel name, the time and like count), as anyone can see them on YouTube;
  • the ability to post a comment reply as the channel, used only for replies the owner has approved.

How the data is used

Only to review the channel's own results and to answer comments on its own videos. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train any general-purpose AI or machine-learning model.

Storage and sharing

Reports and notes are kept in the owner's own files. Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's computer and are not shared. Data obtained through Google's APIs is not transferred to anyone else, except where needed to operate the tool for its owner or where the law requires it.

HSW reader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Its use of YouTube API Services is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting

Comment text copied into the owner's notes is kept only as long as it is needed to answer it and to keep a record of replies. The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which makes the stored tokens useless.

If you commented on the channel

Your comment is handled the same way YouTube shows it to any channel owner. To have it removed, delete it on YouTube, or ask through the contact link below.
